This Privacy Policy explains how Wrinklit, a brand owned and operated by RailQuick Private Limited, handles your personal information when you visit our website, use our platform, create an account, book a pickup, or use our ironing and garment-care services. By accessing or using Wrinklit, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

01. Introduction

Wrinklit is a technology-enabled garment-care and convenience platform operated by RailQuick Private Limited, a company incorporated under the laws of India.

Throughout this policy, the terms "Wrinklit", "we", "our" and "us" refer to RailQuick Private Limited operating under the brand name Wrinklit.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal data responsibly and transparently in accordance with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and applicable rules.

02. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our platform, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal details: Name, mobile number, email address and account information.
  • Address details: Pickup address, delivery address, apartment, building and landmark information.
  • Order information: Garment details, service selections, order history, pickup instructions and preferences.
  • Payment information: Payment status, transaction reference, payment method and billing records.
  • Device information: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system and technical logs.
  • Location information: Location data where you permit location access or provide location details.
  • Communication information: Customer support messages, feedback, complaints and service-related communications.

We collect only information reasonably necessary for the purposes explained in this policy, subject to applicable law.

03. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Processing ironing and garment-care bookings.
  • Arranging garment pickup and delivery.
  • Coordinating with service partners and vendors.
  • Sending order confirmations and service updates.
  • Processing payments, refunds and billing.
  • Providing customer support and resolving complaints.
  • Improving our website, technology and user experience.
  • Detecting fraudulent transactions and preventing misuse.
  • Meeting legal, regulatory and accounting obligations.
  • Sending promotional messages where permitted by law and applicable preferences.

04. Location Information

Wrinklit may use address and location information to identify serviceable areas and coordinate pickup and delivery.

If you grant location permission through your device or browser, we may use that information to help identify your location and improve service convenience.

You can manage location permissions through your device or browser settings. Some location-dependent features may not work without this permission.

05. Orders and Payments

When you book a service through Wrinklit, we process the information required to complete your booking.

Payments may be facilitated through third-party payment gateway providers such as Razorpay or other providers integrated with our platform.

Your payment may be subject to the payment provider's own privacy policy, terms and security procedures.

We may receive payment confirmations, transaction references and payment status information to manage your orders and refunds.

Full card details, UPI PINs and banking passwords should never be shared with Wrinklit customer support.

06. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

We may share relevant information with:

  • Service partners: Ironing professionals, garment-care vendors and operational partners responsible for fulfilling your booking.
  • Delivery partners: Individuals or agencies involved in collecting and returning garments.
  • Payment providers: Payment gateways and financial service providers processing transactions.
  • Technology providers: Hosting, cloud storage, database, analytics and communication providers supporting our platform.
  • Government authorities: Where disclosure is required by applicable law or a lawful order.
  • Professional advisers: Legal, accounting and compliance advisers where reasonably necessary.

Information shared with service providers will be limited to what is reasonably required for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

07. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality and understand website usage.

  • Remembering website preferences.
  • Maintaining session functionality.
  • Understanding website traffic and performance.
  • Improving the overall browsing experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Certain website features may not function correctly if cookies are disabled.

08. Data Security

RailQuick Private Limited takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

These measures may include:

  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms.
  • Secure communication protocols where supported.
  • Restricted access to operational information.
  • Monitoring and security reviews.
  • Appropriate safeguards with technology providers.

However, no online platform or electronic transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

09. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, provide services, resolve disputes and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about the personal data we process.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Request erasure of personal data where legally permissible.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a grievance regarding the processing of your personal information.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in accordance with applicable law.

You may submit privacy-related requests using the contact information provided in this policy. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

Withdrawing consent may affect our ability to provide certain services that depend on the relevant information.

11. Children's Privacy

Wrinklit is intended for individuals who are legally capable of entering into the relevant service arrangements.

We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. Where required, verifiable parental or guardian consent will be obtained before processing children's personal data.

12. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites, payment platforms or third-party services.

Such services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. RailQuick Private Limited is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services before sharing information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, business practices, technology or applicable legal requirements.

Any revised version will be published on this page along with its updated effective date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically to stay informed about how their information is handled.
