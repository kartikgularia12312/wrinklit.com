This page explains how to request deletion of your Wrinklit account. Wrinklit is a brand owned and operated by RailQuick Private Limited. Account deletion requests are handled by our support team.

01. How to Request Account Deletion

Email us from the email address linked to your Wrinklit account. If you cannot access that email address, write to us from another address and include the phone number registered to your account so we can help verify it.

Send your deletion request

Email: privacy@wrinklit.in

Subject: Wrinklit account deletion request

Include the name and registered email address or phone number associated with your account.

For your security, do not include your password, one-time password (OTP), payment card details or UPI PIN in your request. We will never ask you to share these details.

02. What Happens Next

  • We will review your request and may contact you to verify that you own the account.
  • Once verified, we will delete or de-identify your account information that is no longer needed.
  • We will let you know when the request has been processed, or if we need more information.

Deleting your account will end access to it and may remove saved profile details, addresses, preferences and account history. Please mention any active booking in your request; we may need to complete or resolve it before closing the account.

03. Information We May Retain

Some information may be retained where required by law, for legitimate business purposes such as accounting, resolving disputes, preventing fraud or enforcing our agreements, or to complete an outstanding transaction. We limit retained information to what is necessary and keep it only for the applicable period.

For more details about how we handle personal information, please read our Privacy Policy.

04. Need Help?

If you have questions about a deletion request or cannot access your account email, contact our support team at privacy@wrinklit.in.
